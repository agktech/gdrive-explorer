Privacy Policy — GDrive Explorer

Last updated: 20 October 2026
The short version: GDrive Explorer runs entirely in your browser. There is no server on our side — your files, your Google account data and your sign-in tokens never pass through any computer we control. Everything goes directly between your browser and Google.

1. What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app can:

  • View, upload, organise and share files in the Google Drive accounts you connect (Google Drive API).
  • Access only the specific photos and videos you pick through Google's Photos picker (Google Photos Picker API). The app cannot browse your full photo library.
  • See your basic Google profile (name and email address) to show who is signed in.

The app only acts on your explicit clicks — it never scans, copies or uploads anything on its own.

2. Where your data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in your own browser (session storage by default; local storage only if you tick "Remember on this device"). Signing out revokes the token with Google and wipes everything the app stored.
  • Your files stay in your Google Drive / Google Photos. We do not download, copy or keep them anywhere else.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app contains no third-party trackers.

3. What we collect

Nothing. Because there is no backend server, we have no database of users, no logs of your activity, and no way to see your files. If you email us for support, we only keep that email conversation.

4. Third parties

The app communicates directly with Google's APIs (drive.googleapis.com, photospicker.googleapis.com) under Google's own Privacy Policy. No other third party receives your data.

5. Early access note

GDrive Explorer is currently in early access (unverified by Google). Google limits early-access apps to 100 authorised users; your Gmail address is added to the app's authorised-user list solely so Google lets you sign in.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
